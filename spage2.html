Solutions

To address this crisis, a multi-tiered approach is required to transition toward a nature-positive future by 2030:

Step 1: Prioritize the absolute protection of remaining intact areas and biomes—such as primary forests and peatlands.

Step 2: Establish and protect biological stopovers and movement corridors to reduce hazards for migrating species in human-dominated landscapes.

Step 3: Implement large-scale nature restoration projects that empower local communities to manage secondary forests and coastal mangroves, providing both ecological benefits

Step 4: Formally embed Indigenous and local ecological systems into national conservation policies. policies.
